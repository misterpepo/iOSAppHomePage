Privacy Policy

Move Scope - Motion Analysis App

Last Updated: December 18, 2025

Introduction

This Privacy Policy describes how "Move Scope" ("App") collects, uses, and discloses your information. The App is designed to analyze human motion and posture from videos using AI-powered pose detection technology. We are committed to protecting your privacy and ensuring the security of your data.

Key Point: Move Scope is designed to function entirely offline and locally on your device. We do not collect, transmit, or store any personal data on external servers.

Information Collection and Use

All data created within the App is stored locally on your device. The App may process the following types of information:

  • Videos: Videos you select from your photo library for motion analysis.
  • Motion Analysis Data: Joint positions, angles, and skeletal data extracted from videos.
  • Exported Data: CSV files and processed videos with skeleton overlays that you choose to save.

Data Storage

All data you create in the App is stored locally on your device using iOS's secure storage mechanisms. We do not upload, transmit, or back up your data to any external servers or cloud services. You are responsible for backing up your device according to Apple's recommended procedures to prevent data loss.

Photo Library Access

The App requests access to your device's photo library to:

  • Select videos for motion analysis and pose detection
  • Save processed videos with skeleton overlays to your library

These permissions are used solely for providing the App's core functionality. No videos or images are transmitted or stored outside your device.

Data Processing

All motion analysis and pose detection processing occurs entirely on your device using Apple's Vision framework. Your videos are never sent to external servers for processing. This ensures complete privacy of your video content.

Data Sharing

The App includes functionality to export analysis results as CSV files and processed videos. When you choose to share these exports, you determine the recipient and sharing method. We do not have access to or control over the data you choose to share.

Third-Party Services

The App does not integrate with any third-party analytics services, advertising networks, or social media platforms. We do not share your data with any third parties.

Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect the information you create within the App by relying on iOS's built-in security features for local data storage. However, no method of electronic storage is 100% secure. We recommend using device passcodes, Touch ID, or Face ID to secure access to your device.

Children's Privacy

The App is not directed to children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you become aware that a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us at the address provided below.

Your Rights

Since all data is stored locally on your device, you maintain complete control over your information. You can delete any or all data from the App at any time by:

  • Using the deletion features within the App
  • Uninstalling the App from your device

Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page and updating the "Last Updated" date. You are advised to review this Privacy Policy periodically for any changes.
